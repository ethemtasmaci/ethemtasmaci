## 👨‍💻 About

I am a software engineer and technical lead who owns the full lifecycle of a product: R&D and system design, implementation, deployment and day-to-day operations. I work best where heavy data, real-time requirements and geospatial complexity meet, and where a team needs clear architecture, solid engineering practices and a roadmap it can actually deliver.
